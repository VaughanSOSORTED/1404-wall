3. Permanence

Once an inscription transaction has been confirmed, the 1404 Wall, BDAG So Sorted and the website operators may be unable to edit, reverse or delete the underlying blockchain record.

Copies of blockchain information may also be available through nodes, explorers, RPC services, indexes, archives or other third-party services outside the control of this website.

The blockchain is the record. This website is a filtered view.

The 1404 Wall may hide message content from its public interface, including content detected as profanity, abusive language, spam, malicious content or other material the interface chooses not to display.

Hiding content from this website does not delete, edit or alter the underlying blockchain inscription.

A website moderation decision should not be interpreted as a legal determination about the message.

6. Wallets and transactions

You remain responsible for reviewing transaction information presented by your wallet before signing.

Blockchain transactions can involve network fees and may be irreversible after confirmation.

Never provide your seed phrase or private key to the 1404 Wall.

7. Availability and third parties
