⚠ THINK BEFORE YOU POST — YOUR MESSAGE MAY BE PERMANENT

Messages submitted to the 1404 Wall are intended to be recorded on a public blockchain. Once a transaction is confirmed, the message cannot be edited or deleted by the 1404 Wall, BDAG So Sorted or the website operators.

Do not publish personal or private information, unlawful content, threats, defamatory material, or anything you would not want publicly associated with your wallet address.

You are responsible for the content you submit. Blockchain publication does not exempt a user from applicable laws.

Demo preview. On-chain submission is disabled pending contract and network review.

By proceeding when inscriptions are enabled, you will be required to acknowledge the Terms & Disclaimer.

HOW MODERATION WORKS

The blockchain is the record. This website is a filtered view.

Confirmed inscriptions may remain part of the blockchain record even when their message text is not displayed by this website.

The 1404 Wall interface may hide profanity, abusive language, spam, malicious content or other material from its public feed. A display decision does not edit, erase or replace the underlying blockchain record and does not determine whether content is lawful or unlawful.
